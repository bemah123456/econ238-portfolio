Proposition 238

Buy the Houses. Let the River Flood.

Ballot Question

Shall the United States create a $10 billion, ten-year Flood Buyout Fund that, through state agencies modeled on New Jersey's Blue Acres program, offers every owner of a FEMA-designated Severe Repetitive Loss home, within three years, a voluntary purchase at pre-flood fair market value plus relocation assistance; demolishes purchased homes; permanently restricts that land to floodplain habitat and public open space; and publishes yearly counts of offers made, homes bought, and acres restored?

Explanation

About 44,600 homes insured by federal flood insurance have flooded roughly five times each. They are about 0.6% of insured homes but drew about 10% of claims. A YES vote spends $1 billion a year for ten years (about $3 per American per year) to offer their owners a voluntary buyout at pre-flood value, then turns the land into permanent wetland, park and flood storage. Owners who say no keep their homes. Towns lose some tax base and neighbors.
